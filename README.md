## Project Overview

A simulated corporate office setup project demonstrating practical project management skills, including planning, scheduling, budgeting, risk assessment, quality management, stakeholder communication, execution monitoring and project closure.

## Project Tasks & Practical Work

### Task 01 — Project Charter
- Defined project objectives, scope, requirements and expected deliverables.
- Identified stakeholders, project responsibilities and success criteria.
- Established the initial project framework.

### Task 02 — Work Breakdown Structure (WBS)
- Divided the project into manageable phases and activities.
- Organized deliverables into a structured WBS.
- Defined work packages and responsibilities.

### Task 03 — Project Schedule & Gantt Chart
- Developed a project timeline and activity sequence.
- Identified milestones, dependencies and durations.
- Prepared a Gantt chart for schedule monitoring.

### Task 04 — Budget & Cost Management
- Prepared cost estimates and budget allocations.
- Established a simulated budget baseline of PKR 1,400,000.
- Defined cost monitoring and change-control procedures.

### Task 05 — Risk Management
- Identified project risks and assessed their probability and impact.
- Developed a risk register and mitigation strategies.
- Defined risk ownership and response planning.

### Task 06 — Quality Management
- Established quality standards and acceptance criteria.
- Prepared quality assurance and quality control procedures.
- Defined inspection and verification activities.

### Task 07 — Communication Management
- Identified stakeholders and communication requirements.
- Developed a stakeholder communication matrix.
- Defined reporting methods, frequency and responsibilities.

### Task 08 — Project Execution & Closure
- Consolidated project planning and management processes.
- Documented simulated execution monitoring and change control.
- Prepared final project reporting, lessons learned and closure documentation.
